## Architecture and boundary decisions

- One measurement scope binds endpoint A and endpoint B once. `send` means A to
  B and `receive` means B to A in every reporter.
- `semantic_bytes` is the recursive sum of decoded application scalar values.
  It counts fixed-width integer/enum/octet-string values by their normative
  decoded widths and UTF-8 strings by normalized UTF-8 octets. The
  representation-descriptor container and every descriptor member contribute
  zero. Framing, repeats, retransmissions, overlap, unselected representations,
  and lower-layer bytes contribute zero.
- `SemanticAccounting` counts a `(direction, representation_id)` only at its
  first accepted application selection. An identical repeat is idempotent; a
  repeat with a different semantic count fails closed; checked `u64` addition
  prevents evidence-domain overflow.
- `ProtocolStore` durably stores only an opaque 32-octet application-supplied
  immutable-semantics digest keyed by the 32-octet object ID. A matching repeat
  after reopen is idempotent, a conflict is rejected without overwrite, and
  unrelated state remains usable. OceanMail owns normalization, object-ID
  generation, mailbox policy, and receipt meaning.
- Manifest validation includes all decoded scalar storage, including every
  representation descriptor, and rejects the exact 65,537-octet aggregate
  before durable mutation. Pre-allocation use of the input-envelope guard
  remains partial until a conforming manifest decoder is integrated.
- The V08 matrix executes the specification's exact 24 named rows. Its storage
  surfaces are a bounded in-process buffer, a fault-injected representation
  part file, and the durable two-slot store. Every row records the durable state
  before interruption, recovered prefix, first resumed offset, new and duplicate
  payload, receipt state, final digest/ID/decode, and a deterministic trace
  digest.
- `CodecIdentity` makes evidence regeneration accept an explicit future
  ID/revision. Defaults remain implementation-local private-use
  `0xffff0001/2`; no specification allocation is predicted or claimed.
- V01 and V02 remain blocked as public byte evidence despite passing local
  behavior because their bytes use the private codec. V09 remains blocked on
  external M4P review despite passing the local opaque-carrier trace.
