## Files and behavior changed

### Integrated full-width engine path

- `crates/bempic-sim/src/v01.rs` connects full-width descriptor preparation,
  capability negotiation, `SUMMARY`, `OFFER`, explicit
  `REPRESENTATION_DATA`, strict record encode/decode, bounded `DATA`, durable
  prefix writes, integrity/schema decode, atomic commit, and positive receipt.
- Every complete record is sized before encoding and carrier submission.
  Negotiated operation/data ceilings, current carrier record ceiling, total
  contact budget, and directional request budget are applied together.
- Exact accounting now includes directional BEMPIC and carrier octets,
  submitted/delivered representation payload, duplicate payload, useful
  committed bytes, operation totals, quote prediction/error, deterministic
  size checks, and BEMPIC/carrier octets before and through the first body
  payload.
- Contact plans model sender-only, receiver-only, simultaneous, cold/full,
  repeated interruption, source change, replay, truncation, corruption,
  bandwidth, latency, disconnect, byte budget, record ceiling, overhead, and
  elapsed time.

### Full-width durable representation store

- `crates/bempic-store/src/v01_representation.rs` adds two-slot full-width
  state, descriptor binding, contiguous prefix/replay handling, reopen
  reconciliation, staged verification, corruption quarantine, atomic commit,
  exact reread verification, and receipt idempotency.
- Nine named fault points cover descriptor, prefix bytes/state, verified state,
  commit bytes/state, receipt state, and quarantine bytes/state.
- A fully durable uncommitted suffix is verified and committed on reopen before
  positive payload capacity is considered. Zero-length representations work
  with an absent part file.

## Architecture and boundary decisions

The dependency boundary remains:

```text
OceanMail → BEMPIC → M4P → DataLink
```

- The simulator consumes complete opaque carrier records and does not add M4P
  routing, addressing, TTL, custody, fragmentation, network deduplication, or
  DataLink reliability behavior.
- `AuthorizedSource` is an application-supplied deterministic test fact, not
  production authentication.
- SHA-256 binds experimental identities and detects corruption; it is not a
  production cryptographic or authentication profile.
- The opaque-binary decode callback is the deterministic decode used by the
  connected fixture. Manifest codec approval remains blocked.
- DCCL remains prior art only. No DCCL or legally uncertain B2F/LZHUF source was
  added.
- The B1 operation image remains explicitly disposable and unregistered. Exact
  maximum proofs do not freeze or approve a wire format.

## Proposed specification questions

The machine-readable source is
`test-vectors/v0.1-experimental/catalog.json`.

1. For V04, must BEMPIC normalize non-NFC application metadata, or may the
   protocol boundary reject it and require OceanMail preparation to normalize?
2. For V08, is mandatory coverage the Cartesian product of every prefix point,
   endpoint restart mode, and durable storage boundary, or independent coverage
   of each dimension?
3. For V10, what retransmission and receipt state is normative when the final
   positive receipt is lost after durable receiver commit?
4. For V15, which failure codes are normatively retryable, and what sender,
   collection, and representation state must each preserve or reset?
5. May the mandatory bundle publish semantic/state vectors before codec
   allocation while deferring byte-exact wire vectors until a codec is approved?
